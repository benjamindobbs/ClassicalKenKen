Background

About the Project

Classical Technology is a set of learning tools built by and for a Career & Technical Education technology program. It started as one thing — a digital version of a daily KenKen "Do Now" — and has grown into a small suite: adaptive KenKen and SAT practice, visual course skill maps, and a system for grading real work students do in the program's school-based enterprise. Everything is tied together by school-account sign-in so progress follows the student.

The KenKen app began because our team was making the puzzles by hand: creating them, converting to PDFs, printing, cutting, and grading 100+ a day — over 6,000 in a single year. After reviewing the results (below), we built this online version to generate and score puzzles automatically and serve them at each student's level.

The KenKen puzzle engine is a fork of the WebKenKen project by msakuta. Special thanks to Sid Challa for helping set up the Google Cloud application.

Project Objectives

  • Students log in with their school accounts for verification and tracking
  • A score is earned upon completing a puzzle that measures guesses, time, and difficulty
  • Student scores are stored in a server-side database identified by student email
  • Puzzles are served based on student ability (rank)
  • Scores determine a grade relative to puzzle difficulty

How It Works

Signing In & Adapting to You

  1. You sign in once with your school Google account. That keeps you signed in on this device, and links your work to you so your progress is saved.
  2. Each practice app looks at how you've been doing recently and picks what to serve next — a KenKen puzzle at your level, or an SAT question in a skill you're still building.
  3. Every result is saved with the date. Your teacher sees your scores in the Teacher Portal; you see your own on the My Progress page.
  4. You can also use the practice apps without an account by choosing "Play Locally" — nothing is saved in that mode.

If you're in more than one class, a menu at the top of each practice app lets you choose which class you're working for, so you can complete each class's Do Now separately. Only accounts on the school's domain can save data — see the Privacy Policy below.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5/21/2026

Thank you for using Classical KenKen. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. This app complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information We Collect

We request access to the following Google API scope:

  • userinfo.email — to identify and track your progress using your email address

Sign-in is handled by Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0 implicit token flow). The Google access token is verified server-side using the Google OAuth2 tokeninfo API (googleapis.com/oauth2/v1/tokeninfo). We never receive your Google password.

How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your Google Account email address solely for:

  • Providing personalized experiences in our logic puzzle and SAT practice application
  • Storing your scores and progress in our server-side database, keyed to your email address
  • Scaling the difficulty of puzzles and questions based on your performance

Data Storage and Retention

  • Your email address and scores are stored in a SQLite database hosted on our application server
  • A session token (a random UUID) is issued upon sign-in and stored in your browser's localStorage to keep you signed in; it is never shared with third parties
  • Only users signing in from an authorized school domain (hartfordschools.org) are permitted to store data
  • Data may be removed upon request — contact us at the address below

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not disclose your information to any third party. Your data is strictly confidential and used only for the purposes stated above.

Data Security

We implement security measures including encryption and access controls to protect the confidentiality and integrity of your data.

User Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access the data associated with your account
  • Request correction of any inaccuracies in your information
  • Revoke the application's access to your Google Account at any time
